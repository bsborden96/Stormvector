Sources & coverage

Destination weather: Open-Meteo. Official local alerts: National Weather Service. Aurora oval and Kp: NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center. Wisconsin foliage observations: Travel Wisconsin, with reporter and retrieval dates. Minnesota and Michigan foliage observations are not yet connected; no current color percentages are invented.

Destination details are a curated guide, not a live park-access service. Model snowfall is not snow depth, safe lake ice, or a guarantee of winter scenery. Alerts load when you select a place; this site is not a warning alarm.
